From Rajasthan to Your Home: The Journey of Natural Stone

Rajasthan has anchored India's stone masonry legacy since the construction of the Taj Mahal and Amber Palace. At Marmor Designs, every slab undergoes an ancient yet technically refined odyssey from deep desert quarries to your living space.

STAGE 01

Geological Extraction & Block Selection

The journey begins deep within the geological belts of Rajasthan. Our senior quarry prospectors inspect raw rock faces, checking for natural quartz crystal clustering, mineral banding, and zero structural fissures. Only blocks with flawless integrity are extracted using diamond wire saws.

STAGE 02

Gang-Saw Slicing into Jumbo Slabs

At our Kishangarh facility, massive multi-ton stone blocks are mounted on robotic multi-wire gang saws that slice the boulder into 18mm and 20mm uniform slabs over continuous 36-hour cycles cooled by recycled water systems.

STAGE 03

12-Stage Diamond Abrasive Polishing

Each slab passes through a computer-controlled 12-head polishing line. Graded diamond abrasives gently refine the surface from coarse 60-grit smoothing to ultra-fine 3000-grit buffing, bringing out the deep liquid mirror luster of the stone without synthetic wax coatings.

STAGE 04

Reinforced A-Frame Crating & Pan-India Delivery

Finally, slabs are protected with polyethylene film and secured inside fumigated pine timber A-frames equipped with impact-damping rubber feet. Dispatched directly from Kishangarh with door-to-door transit insurance across Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Chennai, and overseas to UAE and UK.
