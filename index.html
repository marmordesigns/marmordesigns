The Marmor Benchmark

What Makes Our Marble The Best Quality
In The Country

Engineered in our state-of-the-art Kishangarh marble hub processing facility using multi-stage Italian reinforcement technology to ensure lifetime structural integrity.

Step 01 · Structural Integrity

Vacuum Epoxy Resin Impregnation

Every natural slab is subjected to deep vacuum chambers where structural epoxy resin penetrates micro-fissures, increasing compressive and flexural strength by over 300%.

Step 02 · Anti-Crack Security

High-Tensile Fiber Mesh Backing

Reinforced with bidirectional fiberglass netting and high-grade polymers, safeguarding the stone against breakage during long-distance transit and precision on-site mitering.

Step 03 · Optical Reflection

12-Head Diamond Line Polishing

Robotic multi-head diamond polishing brings out a 95+ gloss unit liquid mirror finish without applying temporary waxes or superficial chemical coatings.

Step 04 · Monolithic Continuity

Proprietary Jointless Seam Finish

Consecutive dry-lay slab matching combined with custom color-matched stone resin makes seams virtually invisible, creating a continuous monolithic wall.
