O U R   L E G A C Y

Rooted in Kishangarh, Rajasthan — widely acknowledged as Asia's premier stone and marble hub — Marmor Designs represents an unbroken dedication to unearthing the Earth's most breathtaking geological wonders.

We curate, cut, polish, and custom-engineer high-grade translucent agates, crystalline quartzites, deep lapis lazuli, vivid malachite, and majestic onyx. Each curated slab tells a millenia-old story, brought to life with precision diamond gang-saw cutting and artisan book-matching.

Direct Quarry Selection

We source selectively without intermediaries, ensuring flawless density, rich veining, and authentic mineral clarity.

Precision Processing

Bespoke Architectural Supply
