Our Story

Built on a Simple Belief

Marmor Designs began with one idea: natural stone shouldn't be treated as a commodity. Every block of agate or quartz carries its own veining, colour shift and character — our job is to select the right slab for the right space, not just the next one off the shelf.

Today we curate a catalogue of 25+ natural stone varieties, from everyday White Quartz to showpiece Malachite and Lapis, supplying homeowners, architects and fabricators across India with transparent, per-square-foot pricing and a jointless finishing option for a truly seamless surface.

How We Work

From Quarry to Wall

A straightforward process, kept transparent from first enquiry to final polish.

01

Select

Choose a stone from our price list, or tell us the mood you're going for and we'll recommend a few.

02

Measure & Quote

Share your area in sq. ft — we send a detailed quote covering material, jointless finishing and fitting.

03

Fabricate

Slabs are cut and edge-finished to your exact site dimensions before dispatch.

04

Install

Our fitting team installs on-site, checking veining alignment and joint lines before handover.
