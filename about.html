Rajasthan has shaped the world’s grandest stone architectural landmarks for centuries. At Marmor Designs, we fuse this timeless quarry mastery with modern CNC precision to deliver gallery-grade natural slabs across the globe.

The Marmor Story

Where Earth's Ancient History
Meets Contemporary Architecture

Marmor Designs was founded in the historic stone district of Kishangarh, Rajasthan, with a single mission: to demystify luxury stone and deliver authentic, jointless, breathtaking slabs directly from the earth to the world's most discerning homes and hotels.

Unlike commercial suppliers who deal in reconstituted aggregates, our master stone inspectors examine every single boulder at the quarry face. We check for natural crystallization, translucency depth, structural purity and grain alignment before any cutting begins.

The Marmor Standard

Three Pillars of Precision

01

Quarry-Level Curation

Only the top 5% of natural quartz blocks and agate nodules pass our density and visual inspection. We reject micro-fractured or dull lots at the source.

02

Jointless Engineering

Using laser-guided 5-axis diamond bridges and waterjet robotics, we align natural veining seamlessly across vast feature walls and waterfall counters.

03

Transparent Pricing

No ambiguous estimates or hidden transit surcharges. All slab dimensions, square-foot rates and GST-compliant invoices are shared transparently upfront.
