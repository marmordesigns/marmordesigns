Our Story

A B O U T   U S

Est. Kishangarh, Rajasthan

From the Heart of Rajasthan

Nestled in Kishangarh — the stone capital of India — Marmor Designs was born from a singular passion: to bring the world's most extraordinary natural stones to India's finest interiors.

We travel to the ends of the earth — from Brazilian rainforests to African plains, from Himalayan quarries to Mediterranean shores — to handpick stones that speak to the soul. Each slab in our collection is a singular work of art created by millions of years of geological artistry.

Our team of stone connoisseurs, designers and craftsmen work in harmony to ensure every stone is processed, finished and delivered to the exact standards that luxury demands. From raw slab to installed masterpiece, we are with you every step of the way.
