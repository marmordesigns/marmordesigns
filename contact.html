Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How does Pan-India delivery and slab crating work?
Every slab fabricated at our Kishangarh facility is encased in heavy-gauge fumigated wooden A-frames lined with high-density shock-absorbing foam. We provide dedicated transport insurance and door-to-door GPS tracked delivery across all 28 states in India.
What is the Jointless Finish and how is it achieved?
Traditional stonework often suffers from mismatched unsightly grout lines. Our jointless finish involves dry-laying consecutive slab cuts at our factory, color-matching transparent structural resins to the stone's exact mineral pigment, and buffing the seam continuously so the entire wall reads as one continuous monolithic slab.
Can all agate slabs be backlit with LEDs?
Yes! Natural agate, white onyx and crystalline quartz possess natural optical translucency. We calibrate slab thickness to approximately 20mm and provide guidance for back-diffuser acrylic sheets and 3000K–4000K warm LED light panels to achieve optimal glowing depth without hot spots.
Can I inspect the actual slab before purchase?
Absolutely. We believe buying natural stone requires 100% confidence. Once you share your required dimensions, we send high-resolution 4K photos and 360-degree videos of the exact available lot numbers under natural sunlight and artificial gallery lighting before you place your deposit.
What are your payment terms and order lead times?
Standard in-stock slabs are dispatched within 4 to 7 business days from Kishangarh following quality sign-off. Custom waterjet cutting and book-matched book assemblies typically take 10 to 14 days. We accept bank transfers, GST invoices, and international letters of credit for exports.
