Kishangarh — The Stone Capital

Kishangarh in the Ajmer district of Rajasthan is known as the marble and stone capital of India. Home to thousands of stone merchants and craftsmen, it is the epicenter of India's natural stone industry.

Our showroom is located in the heart of this vibrant stone hub, where you can visit, touch and experience our full range of 26 natural stones in person. Our team is on hand to assist with selection, sampling and quotations.

Write to us at manankumarbohra11@gmail.com to schedule a showroom visit or receive a digital brochure.

India's premier curator of natural marble, agate, quartz and rare stone slabs. Handpicked from nature, crafted to perfection — from Kishangarh to your space.
